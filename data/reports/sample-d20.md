# Pre-consultation history (case sample-d20)
*Role-played dialogue. Filled by a language model for the doctor to review. Not a diagnosis.*

## Red flags reported by the patient
- **Fever?** yes. "أيوه عندي سخونية ورعشة"
- **Is the pain constant and steadily getting worse?** yes. "أيوه الوجع ثابت وبيزيد"
- **Do you have a condition, or take medicines, that weaken your immune system?** yes. "أيوه باخد كورتيزون عشان الروماتويد"
- **Is a joint red, hot and very painful, especially with fever?** yes. "أيوه الركبة حمرا وسخنة وبتوجع جدًا"
- **Have you had a recent infection, injection or surgery near the painful area?** yes. "أيوه خدت حقنة في الركبة من أسبوع"
- Patient said no to: Unexplained weight loss?; History of cancer?; Bladder or bowel change?; Optional, doctor decides: do you inject any drugs?; Is there numbness between your legs or around the back passage?; Does the pain get worse with effort, or come with shortness of breath, sweating or dizziness?
- **Not answered (ask the patient):** Do you have numbness, coldness, a colour change or sudden weakness?; Have your hands become clumsy, for example with buttons, or is your balance or walking worse?; Has your bladder or bowel control changed?; With the neck pain, do you have dizziness, fainting, double vision, trouble swallowing or slurred speech?

## Site
- Where does it hurt? Point to the place that hurts you most. **knee**. "ركبتي"
- Is it on the right, the left, or both sides? **left**. "الشمال"
- Is the pain in one place only, or in more than one place? **one_place**. "مكان واحد"
- Can you point to the exact spot with one finger, or is it spread over a wide area? **wide_area**. "أيوه الركبة حمرا وسخنة وبتوجع جدًا"
- Do you also have neck or back pain? **no**. "أيوه عندي سخونية ورعشة"

## History of the problem
- When did it start? **من تلات أيام**. "من تلات أيام"
- Was there an injury? **no**. "لا مفيش وقعة"
- Does pain wake you at night? **yes**. "أيوه بيصحيني ومبيهداش"
- Any swelling? **yes**. "أيوه الركبة حمرا وسخنة وبتوجع جدًا"
- What is bothering you, and why have you come today? **ركبتي ورمت وبقت حمرا وسخنة ومش قادرة أمشي**. "ركبتي ورمت وبقت حمرا وسخنة ومش قادرة أمشي"
- Is the area stiff? **yes**. "أيوه بيصحيني ومبيهداش"
- Does it stop you from walking, climbing stairs, dressing, working or exercising? **لا مقدرش أقف عليها**. "لا مقدرش أقف عليها"
- Have you had a fracture, injury or surgery before, in this place or elsewhere? **yes**. "لا مفيش وقعة"
- Do you have a long-term illness such as diabetes, high blood pressure, rheumatoid arthritis or osteoporosis? **باخد كورتيزون عشان الروماتويد**. "أيوه باخد كورتيزون عشان الروماتويد"
- What medicines do you take now? Do you take cortisone or blood thinners? **باخد كورتيزون وميثوتريكسات، ومبخدش مسيّلات**. "باخد كورتيزون وميثوتريكسات، ومبخدش مسيّلات"
- Do you take cortisone? **yes**. "أيوه باخد كورتيزون عشان الروماتويد"
- Do you take blood thinners? **no**. "لا مبخدش حاجة بالحقن"
- Are you allergic to any medicine? **no**. "لا مفيش تنميل ولا برودة"
- Does anyone in your family have rheumatoid arthritis, cancer, or bone and joint problems? **لا عمري ما اتعالجت من ورم**. "لا عمري ما اتعالجت من ورم"
- What is your job? Does it involve long standing, heavy lifting or repeated movements? **لا مفيش وقعة**. "لا مفيش وقعة"
- Do you play sports or exercise regularly? Which ones? **لا مبخدش حاجة بالحقن**. "لا مبخدش حاجة بالحقن"
- What do you think caused it? What worries you most about it? **بدأ من تلات أيام، فجأة**. "من تلات أيام، فجأة"

## Knee
- Does the knee give way? **no**. "الشمال"
- Does the knee lock, catch or click? **none**. "أيوه الركبة حمرا وسخنة وبتوجع جدًا"
- Is the pain worse going up or down stairs? **no**. "لا مقدرش أقف عليها"
- Do you sit on the floor, squat or kneel, for prayer or work? **no**. "لا مفيش تنميل ولا برودة"
- Can you straighten and bend the knee? **no**. "لا مقدرش أقف عليها"
